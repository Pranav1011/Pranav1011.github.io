# Design Context

Source: Pranav's build brief (`docs/BUILD_BRIEF.md`) and decisions from 2026-09-24.

- **Audience:** hiring managers, recruiters and engineers screening for AI Engineer / agentic AI roles. Recruiters give it ~30 seconds; engineers click into case studies and read the repos.
- **Use cases:**
  - Know the role and niche within one screen.
  - See four projects with proof within two scrolls.
  - Open a case study for depth.
  - Grab the resume or email in one click.
- **Personality:** precise, confident, restrained. Instrument, not ornament. Premium means exact typography, generous space and one memorable element.
- **Visual world:** measurement and instrumentation: experiment logs, timing screens, telemetry traces, calibration plots.
- **Avoid:**
  - dark background with a lime accent
  - cream + serif + terracotta
  - card grids, eyebrow labels, 01/02/03 numbering
  - gradient text, glass, blobs
  - monospace used as a "tech" costume
